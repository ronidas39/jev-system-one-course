So you may copy, change and share them, even for money, without asking.
We still name every author, because it is fair.

How we made the crops: each Commons photo was cut into one picture per egg, by hand-drawn
boxes, then shrunk so the long side is at most 512 pixels. Nothing else was changed.

## The labels

`labels.csv` says, for each photo, if the egg is clean, dirty or cracked.
The course author made these labels by eye, by looking at each photo. They are drafts.
Nobody from an egg farm checked them. Treat them as one careful person's opinion.
Some photos are borderline. The `note` column says which, and why.
The lab uses them to test cut-offs. It does not use them to claim the model is right or wrong.
